01 · The story

Siumora means the thing you were given.

The name descends from Samora — reward, gift. Read a second way it splits into su, the auspicious, and mora. Both readings point at the same moment: something small and precious passing from one hand to another, and staying.

In India that moment is not reserved for weddings. It is a first salary, a cleared exam, a friend's birthday, a Tuesday when you decide you have earned something. Siumora exists for the woman who no longer waits to be given jewellery — she gives it, including to herself.

Positioning Demi-fine, ₹1,500–10,000. Real metal, everyday wear, gift-grade presentation.
Who it is for Women 18–35 across urban India. Self-purchase first, gifting close behind.
The promise Every piece arrives already wrapped as a gift, whoever bought it.
Line to hold Something given, something kept.

02 · The mark

Petal & Kernel — four circles and the reward inside.

Four identical circles are set on the axes of a square, each one half the width of the mark, spaced so that neighbours meet at the centre lines. The petal shapes between them are never drawn — they appear because the circles overlap. A solid kernel, one sixth of the width, is set dead centre.

GridSquare, 1 × 1 (x = mark width)
Circle Ø0.5x, stroke 0.0075x
Kernel Ø0.167x, solid, optically centred
OriginJaali lattice · four-petal offering flower
Reads as A flower offered at a doorway — the oldest gift there is.
And as A stone in its setting, seen from directly above.
And as A jaali screen: four hands closed around one small thing.

Why mulberry The category is split between black-and-gold and cool silver-teal. Mulberry reads gift-worthy and feminine without becoming bridal maroon, and it holds its own against both gold and silver plating in photography.
Rules Brass is a foil, a stroke or a kernel — never a large fill and never a gradient. Body text is always Kohl Ink on Ivory. Mulberry is for one accent per view: a rule, a label, a button.

07 · Pattern

The mark repeats into a jaali screen.

Where it goes Tissue paper, packing tape, pouch lining, order-confirmation email band, the back of the care card, and as a real perforation in the box sleeve so the piece is glimpsed before it is opened.
Rules One tile size per surface, 44px at screen scale, 12mm in print. Always tangent, never overlapping. Single colour only. Never place the logo on top of the pattern — the pattern is what the logo is made of.

SIUMORA · BRAND GUIDELINES09
09 · Voice

Warm, plain, a little bold. Never precious about itself.

We say Something given, something kept. Every piece leaves here wrapped as a gift. Buy it for the Tuesday, not the wedding. Made to be worn, not stored.
We never say Bridal, trousseau, dowry, investment. Luxury, exclusive, elite, premium. Treat yourself, girl boss, slay. Anything that implies she needs permission.
Length Headlines under nine words. Product names under three.
Person We speak as one person to one person. No "our customers".
Detail Always name the metal and the plating. Trust is built in specifics.
